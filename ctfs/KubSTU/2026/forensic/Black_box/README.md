From 12 to 16 September a fixed-wing UAV flew a 62 km gas pipeline corridor in Krasnodar Krai once a day. The fifth sortie ended in a crash. That same evening the operator found the wreck, pulled the SD card and filed an insurance claim: "engine failure, never left the route, here are the logs."

The telemetry is spotless. Yet a farm company off the corridor watched an aircraft circle overhead four days running. And there are only four flight logs on the card, even though there were five sorties.
