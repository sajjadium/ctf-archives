Briefing
Somewhere in Arachne's outer routing layer, a vessel telemetry relay has been running unattended for longer than anyone cares to admit. It was meant to be a quiet internal feed, sensor pings from cargo ships, nothing classified. The kind of system no one watches because nothing interesting ever happens on it. One of our field contacts flagged it. She says there's more behind that feed than hull temperature and barometric drift. She also says the people running it got lazy about what they left reachable. The administrator's still peeking through the logs, so I wouldn't be surprised if they don't even know we're onto them. She's been wrong before. Not often.
