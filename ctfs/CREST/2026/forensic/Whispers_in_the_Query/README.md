CTF7
A workstation was believed to be making ordinary resolver traffic during an incident. The capture also contains a small number of requests to an external resolver that do not quite fit the rest of the network.

Investigate the capture and recover the flag. The flag uses the normal CREST{...} format; submit it as one line.

Everything needed to solve the challenge is in the capture. It can be investigated offline with Wireshark, tshark, or your own analysis scripts. Packet timestamps describe observation order; they are not a promise that application records were sent in a convenient order.
