"ColdLink" services the refrigeration case controllers at a regional supermarket chain. Overnight, something forced every refrigerated case into continuous defrost — dairy, produce, meat, and frozen are all climbing toward spoilage, and the cases will not hold temperature.

You've been given network access to the affected store's ColdLink CaseManager CM-900 controller and a copy of the ColdLink technician toolkit. Work out how to authenticate to the controller, find what is forcing the defrost, and bring all six cases back under the compliance ceiling — and keep them there. The controller is stubborn: a naive fix doesn't stick. Make the cold chain hold, and the controller's compliance status will release proof of the repair.

The toolkit, protocol reference, and a sample historian export are in the download. The controller exposes a control service and a read-only historian feed; see the reference document for ports and commands.
