Miscellaneous
A private collection has been separated from its catalogue. Two archives remain, but neither tells the whole story.

Six works once belonged to the collection. Identify the works and their artists, trace the correct records in the museums' official collections, and recover what the collector left behind. Six Missing Records

Record I

A young woman turns her gaze toward you. Blue and gold frame her face, while a single pearl catches the light. Her painter belonged to the age of the Dutch masters.

Record II

Under the light of a lantern, a man in white stands with arms spread before a firing squad. Madrid remembers the night, and the painter remembered its horror.

Record III

A quiet village sleeps beneath a restless sky. Stars burn, clouds spiral, and a crescent moon watches over a world transformed by a troubled hand.

Record IV

Two lovers hold each other amid gold, patterns, and ornament. Their painter turned intimacy into a symbol and made gold speak.

Record V

A figure stands upon a bridge, hands pressed to its face. Behind it, the sky burns red. More than one version exists, and not every record tells the same story.

Record VI

Two figures cling together, caught between tenderness and death. Their painter's life was brief, his lines unmistakable, and the year of his passing was 1918. The Collector's Warning

A picture may survive a change of frame, yet lose what the eye was never meant to see.

A copy may preserve the scene while forgetting its smallest marks. Keep the originals untouched; a faithful likeness is not always a faithful record.

Six fragments lie scattered, each bearing its own seal. Validate what you recover, then arrange the fragments by the years their artists were born.

The catalogue records hold another part of the answer. Preserve their details, for even a small difference may change the result.

Joined in their proper order, the fragments provide the material needed to derive the key. The catalogue must be accounted for before the cabinet can be opened.

The collector was careful. Be more careful.
