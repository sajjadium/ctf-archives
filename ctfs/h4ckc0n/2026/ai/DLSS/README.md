Author: iparc

Three weeks of planning. One museum. One painting worth stealing.

Our crew was a driver, a lookout, and 4xup, our tech guy. The plan was simple: we get inside, lift the data off the right canvas, and 4xup decodes it back at the hideout. Then he went dark. All he left us was his decoder, model.pt, and one rule he kept repeating: “Always blow it up 4x. And never show anyone the negative.”

The security is mostly bad lighting and worse floors. Get through the halls, look over the frames, and take copies of whatever you can. The place is full of forgeries, and not every piece is worth what it’s hanging for.
