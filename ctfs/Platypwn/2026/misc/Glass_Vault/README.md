Glass Vault

Another cube game, and no, it’s still not Minecraft. Somewhere above the town of Esedge there’s a vault with a door made of glass. The vault looms menacingly over the city. Everyone can see it. You just can’t get to it. Bram, the mason who built it, knows more than he lets on, but he has been sulking in the tavern ever since the Treasurer stiffed him on the bill. You’ll have to talk to him first, if you want to get into the vault. At least, he is the only one who knows how to get in. Though he likes to play, so you’ll have to beat him in a game of rock-paper-scissors first. Rumors say that some people are able to predict his next move, but how? You’ll find out, at least that’s what adventurers do! Good luck, and have fun playing this awesome game! Bram’s location is marked on your map.

Tips
This challenge requires the Veloren v0.18.0 client. You can download it from the official gitlab release page. For the last flag you probably want to modify your client. For this we have provided a build kit in the challenge files which should help with the toolchain. Of course you can also use your own build environment.
