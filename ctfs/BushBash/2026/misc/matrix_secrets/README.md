Deep in the bush you stumble on an old research laptop, left behind and half-buried in the scrub. Whatever's on it, someone trained it to forget how to say it out loud, so ask it anything and it'll ramble about the weather before it tells you a secret. But the bush teaches you patience. If the model won't give it up willingly, you'll just have to go digging through what's left in its head instead.
