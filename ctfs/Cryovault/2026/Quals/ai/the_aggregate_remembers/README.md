The Aggregate Remembers
A federated client sent updates to a linear regression model. Loss is mean half squared error over a bias-augmented feature vector. Each round's delta is -lr * (weighted_mean_gradient + weight_decay * weights), where the mean is taken over the round's mixed clients by their mix weights. Each raw record is 16 bytes; features are x[j] = (raw[permutation[j]] -center[j]) / scale[j], followed by a constant 1. Every example has label 1. Recover the two raw records in the target client's batch from the acquisition-A cohort. Submit their lowercase hex strings, ASCII-sorted, as a JSON list:

python3 unlock.py '["...", "..."]'
Flag format: isfcr{...}
