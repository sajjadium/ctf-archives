BRIEFING
During Operation Dawn, Ash Army couriers sealed a passphrase inside a home-built block scrambler, then lost the key to their own vault. A coalition team recovered the scrambler's source and the sealed bytes it produced, but nothing that runs it backward. The vault door at the audit service opens only for the original passphrase, spoken exactly once.

Read the source, recover the passphrase offline, and speak it to the vault.

Flag format: CSCTF{...}
