A mobile field forge the Ash Army hauls behind the line, stamping out replacement parts on demand: any size, no questions asked. The duty smith keeps a rack of work orders you can open, extend, read back and scrap. Push an order past what the metal can hold, turn the forge's own bookkeeping against it, and bend the next pour to a command of your choosing.
