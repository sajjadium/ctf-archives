For thirty-one years, the keeper of Skarn Point lit his lamp for a ship
that had already been lost. The Board called it grief. The village called
it madness. He said only: "Ask the light."

His official log is on file. But a keeper who knows he is being read
writes two logs --one for the Board, and one for whoever is willing to
look closer.

What was the light really for?
