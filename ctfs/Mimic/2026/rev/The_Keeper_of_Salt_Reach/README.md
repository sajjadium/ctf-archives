The lighthouse at Salt Reach went dark on a night with no storm and no fog.

When the coast guard arrived, the door was bolted from the inside, the lamp was cold, and the keeper's logbook was missing. The only thing left behind was a small sealed program on the keeper's desk, still waiting for input.

The villagers say he trusted no one, and spoke to the sea more than to people.

Whatever he locked away, he wanted it to stay lost.

Find out why.
