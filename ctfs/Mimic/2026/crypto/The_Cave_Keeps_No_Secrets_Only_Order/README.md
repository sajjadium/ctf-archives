THE CAVE KEEPS NO SECRETS, ONLY ORDER

  Fire throws the illusion upon the wall, and the chained call it a world.
  All they have ever touched is a reflection of someone else's walking.

  Chained men argue about the names of passing figures, never the figures.
  A freed one turns his head, and the illusion burns his eyes.

  Dawn waits outside, patient and untroubled by anyone's illusion.
  Every cave keeps its mirror; the shadow is honest, the watcher is not.

  --
  Names come in the order the lamp speaks them; the unspoken keep their old order.
  The teacher is the longest word that every stanza speaks, yet he is seen in a mirror.
  The road is laid across as many rows as the poem has stanzas.
